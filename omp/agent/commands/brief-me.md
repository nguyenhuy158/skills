Brief me on the active work, then continue it.

Report promptly. Use current evidence first; retrieve only a material missing
fact, and tell me before a lookup that will take time.

Cover changes since the last update, the objective, completed outcomes, work in
progress including delegated or background work, remaining work, blockers,
risks, decisions, verification gaps, and the next action. Mark stale, inferred,
unavailable, or unverified claims.

Keep it concise. Do not create a status artifact. Continue immediately, and do
not wait unless the active instructions require my decision or approval.
